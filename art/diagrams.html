Promotion is earned, demotion is a cliff

Per action kind, learned from outcomes — thresholds are never hardcoded per task. Deep insights come before auto-pilot: no measurement, no autonomy.

PROMOTED · one step, never more T2 · veto

reminders now fire unless someone objects in the window. Still measured on every run.

⟶instant · sticky
one “this needed me”
DEMOTED · UNTIL HUMAN RESET T3 · consent

time does not re-promote. Only a deliberate reset reopens the path — and the record restarts from zero.

🟡 the yellow card “this did not need me” is evidence for the review, never a lever. You don’t reach autonomy by reclassifying what gets counted.
⛰ why a cliff complacency drops clear straight into chaotic (Cynefin’s fold). The way up must be a staircase, the way down a cliff.

Reframe, never force

A thread is scoped to a subject and a topic — one person can hold many. With a human counterpart, resampling is not free: repetition has a punitive social cost.

Any reply resets everything — nudging someone who just answered is noise, and a counterpart who replied is never “stalled”. What ascends after a close is a compressed failure summary, never the raw history.
